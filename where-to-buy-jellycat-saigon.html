How to spot an authentic Jellycat

  • The paper hang tag from Jellycat, printed with the design’s name and a barcode.
  • The fabric label sewn into a seam, with the Jellycat logo.
  • The fur is soft and even in colour; eyes, nose and seams are neat and secure.
  • The price: an unusually cheap Jellycat is a warning sign.
  • See it in person before you buy. At Bunniihaus you can check every piece in the shop.
